Methodology, Architecture & Ethics

A detailed technical explanation of the dual-engine pipeline, NLP feature engineering, and live news verification mechanisms.

1. The Limitations of Pure ML Classifiers

Most conventional college fake news projects make a critical architectural flaw: they treat fake news detection as a simple binary classification problem. A machine learning model (e.g. TF-IDF + Naive Bayes or Logistic Regression) merely learns stylistic vocabulary, sensational punctuation, and lexical patterns from static training datasets.

However, real-world truth is an empirical question, not a stylistic one. A completely true news event written in a colloquial tone could be misclassified by an ML model as "fake", while a sophisticated, calmly written piece of state propaganda could fool the classifier as "real". NewsCheck AI solves this by introducing a dual-engine architecture: combining linguistic ML pattern recognition with real-time empirical evidence retrieval.

Benchmark Finding: The classifier scores about 99.6% on the ISOT test set. This measures how well it separates this dataset’s writing styles, not whether a real-world claim is true. That is why NewsCheck AI adds live evidence-based verification.

2. System Architecture

Engine 1: NLP & Statistical ML

  • Leakage Prevention: Automatically strips wire agency headers (e.g., WASHINGTON (Reuters) -) during training to prevent artificial classifier bias.
  • Feature Extraction: Sublinear TF-IDF vectorizer using unigrams and bigrams (10,000 maximum features).
  • Model Selection: Trains and benchmarks Passive-Aggressive Classifier vs. Logistic Regression, selecting the highest F1-score model.

Engine 2: Live Evidence Verification

  • Entity Extraction: Isolates proper nouns, numbers, currencies, and temporal markers (years/dates).
  • Accredited Source Querying: Queries Reuters, BBC News, The Hindu, Times of India, and official government registries.
  • Debunking Detection: Detects fact-check releases and debunk notices from credible newsrooms.

3. Ethical Fact-Checking Guidelines

A Newspaper's Silence is Never Proof of Falsity: If an automated query yields no articles, the system strictly assigns the verdict Unverified — insufficient evidence. We never classify news as fake simply because an article is not indexed.

No Hallucinated Citations: Unlike generative chatbots that can invent imaginary citations and fake URLs, NewsCheck AI only displays real articles returned by authorized APIs with verifiable outbound links.

Transparent Disclaimer: The machine learning classification is explicitly labeled as a lexical pattern score, ensuring users understand that statistics do not replace journalistic due diligence.
